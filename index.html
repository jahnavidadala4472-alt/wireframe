Research & Problem Definition

This section establishes the foundation of the EcoTrack application. By analyzing the core problems users face with municipal recycling, we prioritize the features necessary to build a helpful, user-centric tool. The chart below visualizes the distribution of identified user needs guiding our design.

⚠️ The Core Problem

Users want to recycle more effectively but are confused by local municipal rules, pick-up schedules, and where to take non-standard items like batteries and electronics.

Identified User Needs

Clarity "What can I put in which bin?"
Reminders "When is the next pickup?"
Discovery "Where is the nearest glass/e-waste center?"
Motivation "Is my effort actually making a difference?"

Design Thinking & Next Steps

A review of the methodological approach used to reach these wireframes, and the roadmap for translating these low-fidelity concepts into a high-fidelity, tested product.

Empathize: We started by looking at the confusion surrounding multi-stream recycling. Users want to do the right thing but hit friction points due to a lack of immediate, hyper-local information.

Define: The core value established for the app is "Instant Clarity." Every feature must serve the goal of removing friction from the physical act of recycling.

Ideate: We prioritized the visual Scanner and Map features over social or community features to keep the MVP focused on pure utility. Utility drives initial adoption.

Prototype: The low-fidelity wireframes presented here focus heavily on information hierarchy and touch-target placement rather than visual styling. This ensures the structural logic is sound before investing in high-fidelity assets.

🚀

Next Steps
